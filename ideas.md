# Basketball Tracker - Design Ideas

## Context
Carmel Valley Spring 2026 Basketball League — Grades 3rd-4th Boys team tracker.
Audience: Parents, coaches, and players. Needs to feel energetic, sporty, and easy to navigate.

---

<response>
<idea>
**Design Movement**: Urban Athletic / Sports Editorial

**Core Principles**:
1. Bold typographic hierarchy — large jersey-number-style numerals for stats
2. High-contrast dark court aesthetic with vivid orange/amber accents
3. Card-based layout with strong diagonal slashes separating sections
4. Data-forward: stats and schedules are the hero content

**Color Philosophy**:
- Background: Deep charcoal `#1a1a2e` (night court)
- Primary accent: Blazing orange `#FF6B00` (basketball)
- Secondary: Bright white `#FFFFFF` for text
- Muted: Slate gray `#2d2d44`
- Emotional intent: Energy, competition, focus

**Layout Paradigm**:
- Sticky top navigation with team name and live record badge
- Asymmetric hero with large team name on left, stat cards on right
- Horizontal scrollable game cards for schedule
- Full-width standings table with row highlights

**Signature Elements**:
1. Orange diagonal slash dividers between sections
2. Jersey-number typography (bold condensed numerals) for scores/stats
3. Court-line texture as subtle background pattern

**Interaction Philosophy**:
- Hover on game cards reveals score details with slide-up animation
- Tab switching with underline slide animation
- Smooth scroll between sections

**Animation**:
- Section entrance: fade-up with 0.3s stagger
- Score counters: number roll animation on page load
- Nav: blur-backdrop on scroll

**Typography System**:
- Display: "Barlow Condensed" Bold — for headings, team names, scores
- Body: "DM Sans" — for descriptions, schedules, details
- Accent: "Barlow Condensed" Italic — for labels, badges
</idea>

## Selected Design: Urban Athletic / Sports Editorial (Response 1)

Dark court aesthetic with blazing orange accents, bold condensed typography, and diagonal slash design elements. This creates an energetic, competition-focused feel perfect for a youth basketball league tracker.
